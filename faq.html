Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about research, applications, and our facilities.

Applications & Admissions

What backgrounds do you typically hire for?
Our research involves interdisciplinary applications. We usually welcome students with backgrounds in Biomedical Engineering, Computer Science, Neuroscience, Physics, Medicine, and Electrical Engineering.
Are there opportunities for Bachelor's or Master's thesis projects?
Yes, we regularly offer thesis topics. Please reach out via our contact form specifying your interest and attaching your current transcript.

Facilities & Locations

Where are your lab facilities located?
Our research sites are distributed across three main locations: the Neurocenter of the Saarland University Hospital (Homburg), the Technikum of htw saar (Saarbrücken), and the MediClin Bosenberg Rehabilitation Center.
Can I access the lab on weekends?
Access outside normal working hours requires special permission and completion of advanced safety training protocols due to the nature of our equipment.

Technical & Research

Which programming languages do you use?
Our primary tools for data analysis and neuroimaging are Python and MATLAB. Depending on the project (e.g., Virtual Reality or BCI), C++ or C# (Unity) may also be required.
Are the datasets publicly available?
We support open science. Whenever ethical approvals and privacy laws permit, anonymized datasets are published alongside our research papers on repositories like OpenNeuro.
