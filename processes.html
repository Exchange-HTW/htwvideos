Lab Processes

Standard Operating Procedures and Guidelines for the SNN-Unit.

1. Joining the Lab

Overview of the onboarding process for new researchers, Ph.D. students, and student assistants.

  • Submit application via Contact Form.
  • Initial interview with Principal Investigator.
  • Technical/Background review.
  • Lab safety induction & workspace allocation.

2. Running an Experiment

Steps required before conducting human-subject research or data collection.

  • Draft research protocol.
  • Obtain ethics committee approval (Saarland University).
  • Reserve equipment and lab space via internal calendar.
  • Secure data storage and anonymization protocols.

3. Publishing & Authorship

Guidelines for submitting papers to journals and conferences.

  • Internal review of manuscript by all co-authors.
  • Ensure SNN-Unit affiliation format is correct.
  • Upload preprints to approved repositories (e.g., bioRxiv).
  • Log publication in internal database upon acceptance.
